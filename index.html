About

About me

A quick summary of my background and how I approach the work.

OA

I am a Business Analyst and Data Analyst with experience in fintech sales, customer relationship management, customer onboarding, retention, and business process improvement. My work focuses on helping businesses understand problems, document requirements, improve workflows, analyze data, and make better decisions.

I have practical experience using tools such as Excel, SQL, Power BI, and CRM platforms (for fintech sales and customer relationship management), along with business analysis techniques including stakeholder analysis, process mapping, gap analysis, fishbone analysis, user stories, acceptance criteria, RACI matrix, and business case development.

Approach

My approach

Every analysis is designed to answer one question: what should the business do next?

01

Define the business problem

Start with stakeholders to understand what's really going wrong and why it matters.

02

Analyze & structure the data

Clean, organize, and model the data so it can actually answer the question.

03

Identify key insights

Surface the patterns and opportunities that matter most to the business.

04

Deliver recommendations

Turn findings into clear, actionable next steps — not just a dashboard.
